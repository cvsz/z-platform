# Project Purpose - zwiki

## Goal
Central Knowledge Base & Intelligence Wiki for the Zeaz / zWorkforce Platform.

## Key Questions
1. How do all subdomains, tunnels, and microservices interface?
2. What are the active AI agents and workflows?
3. What are the deployment architecture requirements?

## Scope
**In scope:**
- System architecture & topologies
- AI Agents, MCP Tools, and workflows
- Infrastructure, Cloudflare Tunnels, and Terraform configurations

**Out of scope:**
- Ephemeral test logs

## Thesis
A unified, accessible knowledge repository accelerating autonomous agents and human collaboration.
